Seizure warning

Pages linked below can produce flashing light. Nothing flashes on load. Each one needs a button press, stops after 3 seconds, and does nothing at all if your system is set to reduce motion — except the broken animation_from_interaction, whose defect is ignoring that setting. It does not flash: it slides a panel for 0.6 seconds, and warns before its button.

If flashing content may be unsafe for you or you are unsure, you can still test these: the keyframes and timings are in the page source whether or not they are playing.

WCAG 2.3.1 is not simply "faster than 3Hz". Content passes if it flashes at or below three times per second, or stays under both the general and red flash thresholds. The general threshold is defined over a proportion of the visual field, which depends on screen size and viewing distance — something a page cannot know. A tool can measure frequency and the fraction of the viewport; it cannot measure the visual field. flash_small_area exists to make that limit explicit, and expects cannot-tell.
