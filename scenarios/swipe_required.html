swipe_required — broken

Changing fare needs a horizontal swipe on the card: the direction of the pointer path picks next or previous. The arrow keys also work, but a single tap or click does nothing, so there is no single-pointer way to do it.

Swipe left or right on the card to see another fare. From the keyboard, focus the card and press the Left or Right arrow.
