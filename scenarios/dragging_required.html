dragging_required — broken

Reordering the trip requires dragging a handle. The handles also respond to the arrow keys, so the keyboard can do it, but a single tap or click does nothing: there is no way to reorder with a single pointer and no dragging.

Drag a handle to change the order. From the keyboard, focus a handle and press the Up or Down arrow.
