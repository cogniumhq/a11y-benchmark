forced_colors — broken

The selected class is shown by a background fill and white text, which a forced-colours palette replaces, so the selection disappears in that mode. As authored it is a 7.5:1 fill, a lightness difference, on items that are not UI components. Expected outcome under WCAG 2.2: pass for 1.4.1 and 1.4.11 — WCAG 2.2 has no criterion for surviving a forced-colours theme. Kept as a robustness scenario.
